Chapter 1

Introduction

What the portal does and the key terms you need to know

1.1 What is the Bulk Indent Portal?

The Customer Bulk Indent Portal is a web application that lets authorised NRL customers create and manage bulk indents against their open SAP sales orders. A bulk indent is an internal record that captures how a customer's ordered goods will be delivered — specifically, which third-party agents or transporters are responsible for each quantity of goods.

The portal connects directly to the SAP system, so every indent and allocation you create here is immediately visible to the NRL logistics team.

🎯 What you can do

  • Search and view your open sales orders
  • Create bulk indents against sales orders
  • Assign agents and carriers to each indent
  • Allocate specific quantities per agent
  • Track assignment progress in real time
  • Add and manage your agent master records

👥 Who uses this portal?

  • Customer representatives who place bulk orders
  • Logistics coordinators assigning agents
  • Any staff authorised to manage agent allocations on behalf of the customer

1.2 Key Concepts

TermWhat it means
Sales Order An order created in the SAP system. It contains one or more items (products, quantities, prices). You cannot edit the sales order here — only view it.
Bulk Indent A record you create in this portal against a sales order. It captures delivery details (location, date, shipping mode) and the agent/carrier assignments.
Agent A third-party representative who physically handles and delivers goods on behalf of the customer.
Agent Allocation The act of assigning a specific agent to handle a portion (or all) of the ordered quantity.
Shipping Condition CL (Collected) – goods are collected by the agent from NRL's location.
DL (Delivered) – goods are delivered by NRL to the destination.
Assignment Status Shows how much of a sales order has been covered by bulk indents: Unassigned Partially Assigned Fully Assigned
Transporter / Carrier The logistics company or vehicle operator who physically transports the goods. Each carrier has a GSTN number registered in the system.
Chapter 2

Logging In

How to access the portal and authenticate with your SAP credentials

2.1 Login Screen Overview

When you open the portal for the first time, you will see the Login screen. This screen asks for the same SAP username and password you use to log in to other SAP systems at NRL.

ℹ️
Note: If you are accessing the portal through the SAP Business Server Pages (BSP) link provided by your administrator, the system may log you in automatically using your existing SAP session — you will be taken directly to the main dashboard without seeing the login page.

2.2 How to Sign In

1
Enter your SAP Username Type your SAP user ID in the Username field (e.g., JSMITH).
2
Enter your Password Type your SAP password. The characters will be hidden for security.
3
Click "Sign In" The system will verify your credentials with the SAP backend. This usually takes 2–5 seconds.
4
You are taken to the main dashboard On success, the portal opens the Bulk Indent Portal main screen automatically.
⚠️
Wrong credentials? A red error message will appear below the Sign In button. Double-check that Caps Lock is off and that you are using your SAP credentials, then try again. If you have forgotten your password, contact your SAP system administrator.

Session Behaviour

Once signed in, your session is kept active for the duration of your browser session. If you close and reopen the browser tab, you may be asked to log in again. The portal automatically refreshes its security token every 10 minutes in the background to keep your session alive while the page is open.

Chapter 3

Main Dashboard – Sales Documents

Searching for and reviewing your open sales orders

After login you land on the Bulk Indent Portal dashboard. The page is split into two main areas via tabs at the top:

  • Sales Documents — view and manage your SAP sales orders (three sub-tabs).
  • Bulk Indents — view all bulk indents you have already created.

3.1 Searching for Sales Orders

Sales orders are not loaded automatically. You must specify a date range first.

1
Open the "Sales Documents" tab and ensure you are on the All Orders sub-tab.
2
Set the date range. By default, the From Date is two days before today and To Date is today. You can click either date field to pick a different date from the calendar.
3
Click "Search". The portal fetches all sales orders created within that date range and displays them as cards.
💡
Tip: If you do not see an order, try widening the date range. Sales orders appear based on their creation date in SAP.

3.2 Understanding Each Order Card

Each search result appears as a card. The card header always shows:

InformationDescription
Order NumberThe SAP sales order number (e.g., 224200).
Customer ReferenceYour internal purchase order or reference number.
Customer Ref. DateDate on the customer's purchase order.
Customer CodeYour SAP customer number.
Delivery LocationWhere the goods are to be delivered.
Delivery DateRequested delivery date from the sales order.
Shipping ConditionCollected (CL) or Delivered (DL)
Total AmountNet value of the order with currency.
Assignment StatusHow much of this order has been covered by bulk indents (see below).

Click on a card to expand it and see the list of items (materials, quantities, unit prices, plants) within that order. Click again to collapse it.

3.3 Understanding Assignment Status

StatusWhat it meansAction available
Unassigned No bulk indent has been created for this sales order yet. Click Manage to create one.
Partially Assigned A bulk indent exists but some item quantities are still unallocated to agents. Click Manage to add more agent allocations.
Fully Assigned All quantities have been allocated. No further action needed. The Manage button is disabled. View only.
Chapter 3 (continued)

Filtering & Sub-Tabs

Narrowing down the orders you see

3.4 Filtering Orders

After a search returns results, a Filters panel appears above the order cards. You can narrow the list using:

FilterOptionsEffect
Shipping Condition All / Collected (CL) / Delivered (DL) Shows only orders with the chosen shipping mode.
Assignment Status All / Unassigned / Partially Assigned / Fully Assigned Shows only orders matching the chosen assignment state. Available on the All Orders sub-tab only.
ℹ️
Filters are applied instantly — no need to click Search again.

3.5 The Three Sub-Tabs Explained

📋

All Orders

Shows every sales order in the date range, regardless of assignment status. Use this tab as your starting point. Both filters (Shipping Condition and Assignment Status) are available here. Expandable order cards show individual line items with full quantity and price details.
🔶

Partially Assigned

Shows only orders that have a bulk indent but still have unallocated quantity remaining. When you expand a card, the Remaining Quantities table shows what is still outstanding for each item. Quantities highlighted in orange have some remaining; those in red show zero (fully allocated for that line).
✅

Fully Assigned

Shows only orders where every item quantity has been allocated. This is a read-only view — no Manage button is shown. The expanded card shows two quantity columns: QTY (Unassigned) and Total QTY, confirming full coverage.
Chapter 4

Bulk Indents Tab

Viewing all bulk indents you have already created

Click the Bulk Indents tab at the top of the main dashboard to see a list of all bulk indents your account has created. This view is available without doing a search — it loads automatically.

What You See

Orders are displayed as cards and grouped by their parent sales document. The group header shows the sales document number, shipping condition, and net value so you can quickly identify which sales order each indent belongs to.

Each bulk indent card shows:

FieldDescription
Bulk Indent No.Unique number assigned by the system when the indent was created.
Status badgeCreated – indent exists but no agents assigned yet.
Agents Allocated – at least one agent allocation has been saved.
Customer CodeYour SAP customer number.
Customer ReferenceYour purchase order reference.
Reference DateDate from your purchase order.
Shipping MethodCollected (CL) or Delivered (DL).
Delivery LocationDelivery destination (shown if applicable).
Delivery DateRequested delivery date (shown if applicable).

Opening an Existing Bulk Indent

Click anywhere on a bulk indent card (or the View Details button) to open it in the Manage Bulk Indent screen. From there you can view all details, add or remove agent allocations, or delete the indent if needed.

Refreshing the List

Click the Refresh button at the top of the Bulk Indents panel to reload the list from SAP. This is useful if another team member has just created an indent on the same account.

💡
Tip: If you just created a new bulk indent and do not see it here, click Refresh.
Chapter 5

Creating a Bulk Indent

Step-by-step guide to converting a sales order into a bulk indent

5.1 Opening the Manage Screen

From the All Orders or Partially Assigned sub-tab, find the sales order you want to work on and click the Manage button (shown inside the expanded card). You will be taken to the Manage Bulk Indent screen.

⚠️
The Manage button is disabled (greyed out) if the order is already Fully Assigned. No further indents can be created against a fully assigned order.

5.2 Filling In Order Details

The Manage Bulk Indent screen has two columns. The left column shows order details and action fields. The right column shows the items in the order as a scrollable carousel.

Left Column – Fields to Complete

FieldRequired?What to enter
3rd Party Agent Required Select whether a third-party agent will be involved in the delivery.
For CL orders: Choose Yes or No.
For DL orders: The field is automatically set to N/A (not applicable) because DL deliveries are handled by NRL directly. You cannot change this.
Delivery Location Required For DL and CL orders: This is pre-filled from the sales order and locked (read-only).
For orders without a location in SAP: Type the delivery destination manually.
Delivery Date Required For DL and CL orders: Pre-filled from the sales order and locked.
For other orders: Pick the date from the calendar.

Right Column – Order Items Carousel

Use the navigation arrows (or swipe on touch screens) to scroll through each item in the order. For each item you can see the material code, description, quantity, unit price, and plant. For CL orders, a progress bar shows how much of the quantity has already been allocated to agents.

5.3 Creating the Bulk Indent

1
Complete all required fields (3rd Party Agent, Delivery Location, Delivery Date).
2
Click "Create Bulk Indent". The button is located at the bottom of the left column.
3
The system creates the indent and assigns it a Bulk Indent No. A green success badge appears confirming the creation.
4
If you selected "Yes" for 3rd Party Agent, the Agent Allocations section appears below. Proceed to Chapter 6 to assign agents.

5.4 Deleting a Bulk Indent

If you need to remove a bulk indent (for example, due to a mistake), click the Delete Order button (red) visible in the left column when the indent is open. The system will show a confirmation dialog before deleting.

⚠️
Deletion rules:
• You can only delete the most recent bulk indent for a given sales order. If a newer indent exists, delete that one first.
• You cannot delete an indent that already has agent allocations. Delete all agent allocations first (see Section 6.4), then delete the order.
Chapter 6

Agent Allocations

Assigning agents and quantities to your bulk indent

Agent allocations appear at the bottom of the Manage Bulk Indent screen after a bulk indent has been created with 3rd Party Agent = Yes. Each allocation assigns one agent and one transporter to handle a portion of the ordered goods.

6.1 Adding an Agent Allocation

1
Click "Add Agent" (blue button in the Agent Allocations header). A new blank allocation panel expands below.
2
Select an Agent from the Agent dropdown. The dropdown lists all agents registered for your customer. If you need to add a new agent, go to Add Agent first (see Chapter 7), then come back.
3
Select a Loading Date. This is the date on which the goods will be loaded onto the transporter's vehicle. It must be today or a future date.
4
Select a Carrier from the Carrier dropdown. Once selected, the carrier's GSTN number is automatically filled in (read-only).
ℹ️
You can add multiple agent allocations to a single bulk indent by clicking "Add Agent" again for each additional agent.

6.2 Entering Item Quantities

Below the agent and carrier details is an Item Allocations table listing all line items in the order. For each item:

ColumnWhat it shows
ItemSAP item position number.
MaterialMaterial/product code.
DescriptionProduct name or description.
Order QtyTotal quantity on the sales order for this item.
Allocate QtyYou enter this value. Type the quantity you want this agent to handle for this item.
UoMUnit of measure (e.g., PCS, MT, KG).
⚠️
Quantity validation: The quantity you enter for an agent cannot exceed the remaining available quantity for that item. If you exceed the limit, the field turns red and an error message appears — reduce the value before saving.

For CL orders, a progress bar on each item in the right-hand carousel updates as you enter quantities, showing you how much of the total is now allocated.

6.3 Saving Agent Allocations

Once you have filled in all agent details and quantities, click "Save Agent Details" (green button in the Agent Allocations header). The system saves all allocations in one go. A success message confirms the save.

💡
Tip: You can save partial allocations and come back later to add more agents. The indent remains Partially Assigned until all quantities are covered.

6.4 Deleting an Agent Allocation

To remove an agent allocation, click the trash icon (red delete button) inside the allocation panel header. A confirmation dialog will appear. Click Delete to confirm. The system removes the allocation and restores the previously allocated quantity back to the available pool.

Chapter 7

Managing Agents

Adding new agents and maintaining your agent master list

Agents are the third-party representatives you assign to bulk indents. Before you can allocate an agent to an indent, the agent must exist in the system. To manage agents, click the "Add Agent" button in the top-right corner of the main dashboard header.

7.1 Adding a New Agent

The Add Agent Details screen has two sections: a form on the left to create a new agent, and a table on the right listing all existing agents for your account.

1
Agent ID is pre-filled automatically. The system generates the next available ID — you do not need to type it.
2
Enter Agent Name — full name of the agent (max 35 characters).
3
Enter Email Address — must be a valid email format (e.g., agent@example.com).
4
Enter Mobile Number — exactly 10 digits, no spaces or special characters.
5
Enter Address — the agent's full address (max 225 characters). The text box expands as you type.
6
Click "Save". A confirmation dialog appears. Review the details and click OK to create the agent record.

On success, a toast notification confirms "Agent saved successfully." The form clears automatically so you can add another agent if needed. The table on the right refreshes to include the new agent.

7.2 Searching Existing Agents

The Existing Agents table on the right lists all agents associated with your customer account. Use the search bar above the table to filter by any combination of Agent ID, Name, Email, or Mobile Number. The list filters as you type.

ColumnWhat it shows
Agent IDSystem-generated unique ID.
Agent NameFull name in title case.
Email AddressAgent's email (in lowercase).
Mobile Number10-digit phone number.
AddressAgent's address in title case.
CustomerThe customer code this agent belongs to.
Created OnDate the agent record was created.

7.3 Deleting an Agent

Click the trash icon in the Actions column of the agent you want to remove. A confirmation dialog will appear asking you to confirm. Click Delete to permanently remove the agent.

⚠️
You can only delete agents that belong to your customer account. The delete icon will not appear for agents belonging to other customers (even if they are visible in the table).
⚠️
Deleting an agent does not automatically remove existing allocations where that agent has been used. Coordinate with the logistics team before deleting an agent that has active allocations.
Chapter 8

Common Messages & Troubleshooting

What error and success messages mean, and what to do about them

Success Messages

MessageWhat happened
"Agent saved successfully."Your new agent record was created in SAP.
"Agent [ID] deleted."The agent record was permanently removed.
"Agent allocations saved successfully."All agent allocations for this indent were saved to SAP.
"Agent allocation deleted and quantities updated successfully."The allocation was removed and quantities are now available again.
"Order [No.] and its items have been deleted successfully."The bulk indent was permanently deleted.

Error Messages and What To Do

MessageCauseAction
"Invalid username or password." Wrong credentials at login. Check Caps Lock, retype credentials. Contact SAP admin if forgotten.
"Your session has expired. Please log in again." The SAP session timed out. Refresh the page and log in again.
"Quantity (X) exceeds available limit (Y)." You entered more than the remaining available quantity for an item. Reduce the value to Y or less.
"Loading Date cannot be a past date." The loading date you entered is before today. Select today or a future date.
"Cannot delete order with existing agent allocations. Please delete all agent allocations first." You tried to delete a bulk indent that has agents assigned to it. Delete all agent allocations (Section 6.4) first, then delete the order.
"Only the latest order can be deleted. Latest Order: X. Current Order: Y." You tried to delete an older indent when a newer one exists for the same sales order. Open and delete Order X first, then return to delete Order Y.
"This order is read-only because a newer order exists..." A yellow warning bar at the top of the Manage screen. The indent you opened is not the latest for this sales order. Navigate back and open the newest indent (shown in the warning message) to make changes.
"No sales documents found for the selected date range." No orders exist in SAP for the dates you searched. Widen the date range and search again.
"Could not load orders..." / "Something went wrong..." Network or SAP connectivity issue. Wait a moment and try again. If the problem persists, contact your system administrator.

Quick Reference: End-to-End Workflow

Step 1 — Log In
Enter SAP username and password on the Login screen.
Step 2 — Search for a Sales Order
Set a date range and click Search on the All Orders tab.
Step 3 — Open the Manage Screen
Find an Unassigned or Partially Assigned order and click Manage.
Step 4 — Fill in Order Details
Select 3rd Party Agent, confirm Delivery Location and Date.
Step 5 — Create Bulk Indent
Click Create Bulk Indent. A Bulk Indent No. is assigned.
Step 6 — Add Agent Allocations (if 3rd Party = Yes)
Click Add Agent, select agent, loading date, carrier, enter quantities per item.
Step 7 — Save Agent Details
Click Save Agent Details. The order moves to Partially or Fully Assigned status.
